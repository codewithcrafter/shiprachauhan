Core Skills & Expertise

Technical, On-Page & Off-Page SEO

Technical SEO, on-page SEO and off-page SEO all need to be optimised to get good rankings. Technical SEO involves improving site speed with crawlability, indexation and Core Web Vitals. On-page SEO involves improving content, structure, keywords and internal links. Off-page SEO involves improving site authority through quality backlinks, digital PR and brand mentions. If a website isn’t fast with good content or well written but not crawlable they won’t rank well. We ensure all 3 are optimised on all projects.

Local SEO, Google Business Profile & Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)

Local businesses will be competing in a very niche market. Local SEO will involve improving your Google Business Profile, citations, reviews and location pages. But a new part of SEO is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO). This involves optimising your content and entity signals to make sure tools such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity understand and reference your brand. This uses traditional SEO principles.
