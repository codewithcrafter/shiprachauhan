Ecommerce Product Page SEO Optimisation & Product Listing Optimisation

Product page SEO is the highest impact part of your Ecommerce SEO. Our Ecommerce SEO services in Delhi will ensure all your product pages are unique, fully optimised and provide exactly what your customers want in order to convert. Product Structured Data & Product Schema Markup

Structured data will allow Google to understand exactly what the listing is. This includes price, availability, rating, brand etc. Product schema will allow your product pages to gain rich results within search. Customers will see stars, price and availability. If this isn’t done your listings will look dull compared to competitors.

Ecommerce Keyword Research & Product Copy Optimisation

Ecommerce keyword research will ensure you are targeting the right customers. Searches like “buy running shoes online” have an intent to buy. Whereas “how to choose running shoes” doesn’t. Our Ecommerce SEO services in Delhi will ensure your product page copy is targeted to the correct stage of the buyers journey. This ensures you have clear spec, honest benefits and answers to any questions customers may have. This will help your product rank but also convert.

Image SEO, Customer Reviews & Conversion Optimisation

More people are discovering products through visual searches. Therefore our Ecommerce SEO services in Delhi will ensure your image file names, alt text and compression are all set. Lastly we will make small changes to your site to ensure your customers convert from the traffic you are gaining. Structure at scale

Ecommerce Category Page SEO & Faceted Navigation

High-quality Ecommerce SEO services in Delhi will ensure your category pages are optimised. These are usually optimised for the highest volume commercial keywords. They are often left with just a heading and grid. We will ensure your category pages have great introductory content, internal linking and clean URL structure. This will allow your high demand pages to rank.

Faceted Navigation SEO & Canonical URL Management

If you have filters such as size, colour, price and brand you could have thousands of almost duplicated URLs. We will manage canonical URLs to ensure Google knows which page should be ranking. We will also ensure you know which filtered pages should be added to your index.

Large Site Crawl Budget Management

Large sites will have a limited crawl budget. This means we will ensure we aren’t wasting any crawls on low value pages. We will prioritise crawling the most important categories and products.

How to Fix Duplicate Content on Ecommerce Sites

Duplicate content will usually be caused by having multiple URLs to reach the same product. Or the same description for variants. This can be fixed by ensuring you have unique content, canonical tags are correctly implemented and URL parameters are correctly handled.

Technical Performance, Core Web Vitals & App Optimisation

The architecture of your Ecommerce platform will directly impact your Core Web Vitals. With Shopify you could be adding extra app scripts slowing your site down. With WooCommerce you could have heavy themes and plugins. Slow product and checkout pages will cause you to lose rankings and sales. Often fixing these technical issues will have more impact per hour than adding more content.
