## Citeable content, Markdown structure and AI friendly crawlers.

*Technical Groundwork*

### Citeable content

Citeable content
 AI tools rarely cite entire pages so we will optimise your key sections to ensure each paragraph is standalone with clean H1, H2 and H3 headers in line with markdown structure. We’ll create short definitions, lists and labelled comparisons to give the models clean content to pull through.

This is where the markdown hierarchy comes in. Clean, logical heading structure (H1 → H2 → H3, in order, without skipping levels) helps AI crawlers parse what belongs under what topic. A page with a messy heading hierarchy — H2s that jump straight to H4s, or headings used for visual styling rather than structure — makes it harder for these systems to understand which passages answer which questions.

AI friendly crawlers
 Ensuring your page is crawlable is essential. We will check your robots.txt file, meta instructions, render and server responses to ensure GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and other AI crawlers can access your pages. We will also fix any issues blocking them whilst keeping your pages private.

This is part of all projects we will complete as part of our generative engine optimisation services in Delhi. We need to ensure the content is optimised and accessible.
