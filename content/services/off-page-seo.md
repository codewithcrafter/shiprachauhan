## On Page vs Off Page SEO – Why Backlinks are Important

*On Page vs Off Page*

On page SEO is all about optimising your site. This includes optimising your content, titles, structure, speed and internal links. Off page SEO is all about gaining trust from external sources. The best part of off page SEO is backlinks. Often a well optimised page will lose out to a lower quality page with backlinks from other sites.

Both work together. If you have quality on page SEO there will be a reason why people will link to your site. If you have quality backlinks your pages will have the authority to rank. This means we see off page SEO services Delhi companies as part of a larger growth strategy.
