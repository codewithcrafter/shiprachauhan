## Local SEO vs Organic SEO: How to Rank in Google Map Pack Delhi

*Local vs Organic*

While organic SEO and local SEO are related, they aren’t the same job. Organic SEO helps your website rank for searches across the country. Local SEO helps your business appear when someone nearby searches for a dentist, baker or electrician in Karol Bagh, Rohini or Vasant Kunj.

Google will look at your Google Business Profile, reviews, citations and how close you are to the searcher for the map results. Organic results will look at your content, backlinks and site authority. Often, businesses that only do one will lose out to those doing both.
